Four ways Sathi looks after you

Natural voice companion

Chat in everyday words. Ask questions, share feelings or hear a story. It listens and talks back.

Smart daily reminders

Medicines, meals, water and appointments. Add or change them by simply speaking.

Well-being check-ins

Sathi asks how you feel, offers kind company when you are lonely and suggests calling someone you trust.

Family safety alerts

Ask for help with one tap or one sentence. Family you have chosen, with their consent, is notified.

Built for people who find apps hard

Sathi means “companion”. It is made for seniors and the families who worry about them. Everything is voice first, with large buttons and clear text.

Alerts go only to family members you add and who have your consent. Sathi is a companion, not a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
